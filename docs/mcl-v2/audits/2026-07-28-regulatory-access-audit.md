## MCL v2.0 — UK regulatory, HTA and NHS-access ledger

**Audit date:** 28 July 2026
**Source boundary:** live official MHRA, NICE, NHS England, Scottish Medicines Consortium, All Wales Therapeutics and Toxicology Centre/AWMSG, and Northern Ireland HSCNI/SPPG sources. Marketing authorisation, HTA recommendation and operational funding are reported separately.

### Headline conclusions

- **Routinely commissioned in England:**
  - VR‑CAP/bortezomib combination for untreated, transplant-unsuitable MCL — **TA370**.
  - Ibrutinib monotherapy after **exactly one previous line** — **TA502**.
  - Zanubrutinib monotherapy after **exactly one previous line** — **TA1081**, baseline funding recorded from 9 August 2025.
- **England managed access:** brexucabtagene autoleucel remains presented by NICE as a **Cancer Drugs Fund** recommendation under **TA677**, rather than an unrestricted routine recommendation.
- **Licensed but not presently recommended/commissioned nationally for MCL:** acalabrutinib–BR, acalabrutinib monotherapy, pirtobrutinib, lisocabtagene maraleucel and lenalidomide.
- **TRIANGLE ibrutinib:** licensed, but NICE’s current June 2026 draft recommendation is negative; no final TA or national commissioning entitlement exists.
- **Ibrutinib–venetoclax for relapsed MCL:** no current MCL marketing authorisation; NICE appraisal **GID‑TA10774/ID3879** has been suspended since 20 January 2026.
- **Glofitamab:** an MCL NICE topic is awaiting development, but the current GB Columvi licence does **not** include MCL.
- **Epcoritamab:** current GB licence does **not** include MCL and no MCL NICE topic was found.
- England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland must not be treated as a single access jurisdiction.

## 5. Northern Ireland — HSCNI/SPPG audit

Northern Ireland does not acquire an automatic access entitlement merely because NICE has issued guidance; HSCNI/SPPG publishes local service notifications.

- The official SPPG recommended-TA register was live and last modified **9 July 2026**:
  [Recommended technology appraisals](https://online.hscni.net/our-work/nice/recommended-technology-appraisals/)
- **TA677** has a specific HSCNI service-notification record, created 14 October 2022 and updated 29 April 2026:
  [HSCNI TA677 record](https://online.hscni.net/wpfd_file/ta677/)
  This supports local recognition of the NICE managed-access route, but should not be recast as unrestricted routine commissioning.
- **TA370** also has an HSCNI record:
  [HSCNI TA370 record](https://online.hscni.net/wpfd_file/ta370/)
- No directly retrievable HSCNI service-notification record was demonstrated in the documented audit for **TA502** or **TA1081**. This is an unresolved implementation gap, not proof that treatment is unavailable.
- No Northern Ireland national MCL route was demonstrated for TRIANGLE ibrutinib, acalabrutinib–BR, acalabrutinib monotherapy, pirtobrutinib, lisocabtagene, lenalidomide, ibrutinib–venetoclax, glofitamab or epcoritamab. Such use would require local commissioning/individual funding, trial or another explicitly documented route.

## 6. Material unresolved conflicts and cautions

1. **TA677 review/commissioning transition:** NICE still describes TA677 as CDF managed access. NHS England list v1.405 still contains KTE01 forms and managed-access fields, but the extracted table also contains “notice of removal served” wording whose row alignment is not unambiguous in PDF text extraction. Do not claim baseline commissioning or closure until NICE publishes the review and NHS England issues an unambiguous replacement/removal record.
2. **Acalabrutinib–BR overdue NICE field:** GID‑TA11091 still displays expected publication 4 June 2026 and “in progress” on 28 July 2026, despite that date having passed. The only substantive recommendation available is the non-final February consultation draft.
3. **TRIANGLE:** the authorised regimen is more precise than shorthand “TRIANGLE ibrutinib”: ibrutinib accompanies R‑CHOP, not the alternating R‑DHAP/R‑DHAOx cycles, and continues as monotherapy. The current NICE recommendation is only a draft.
4. **Licence–funding gaps:** acalabrutinib monotherapy, pirtobrutinib and lisocabtagene have MCL licences but no demonstrated final national NHS England recommendation.
5. **NICE ID conflict for lisocabtagene:** the title of GID‑TA11930 says **ID6695**, while the page’s ID field says **12299**; preserve both until NICE corrects or explains the record.
6. **BTKi switching:** NHS England’s current forms permit intolerance transfers between ibrutinib and zanubrutinib in the absence of progression. They do not support routine switching between covalent BTK inhibitors after BTKi progression.
7. **Bispecifics:** existing DLBCL/follicular lymphoma authorisations or Blueteq forms for glofitamab and epcoritamab must not be extrapolated to MCL.
8. **Local/trial access:** absence of a national recommendation does not preclude clinical-trial, compassionate/early-access or individual-funding access, but these are not routine NHS entitlements.
