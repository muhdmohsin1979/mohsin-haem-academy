Venous access proforma — PICC versus PIV

Scored route selection, pre-PICC safety checklist and the eight mandatory PIV precautions for lymphoma chemotherapy.

Download the proforma (PDF, 2 pages, A4)

Version 1.0 · April 2026 · reissued for external reference September 2026

What is in it

  • Four scored domains — regimen vesicancy, venous access quality, patient risk factors and monitoring capacity — with decision bands 0–2, 3–5 and ≥6.
  • A mandatory-PICC rule for continuous vesicant infusion of 24 hours or more, and a monitoring override.
  • Seven-item pre-PICC insertion safety checklist.
  • Eight mandatory PIV precautions for vesicant administration, with Visual Infusion Phlebitis scoring.
  • Evidence legend citing the source guideline or study for every threshold.

How to use it

This is a local decision tool developed for a single UK district general hospital haematology service alongside a 2025 service evaluation. It has not been externally validated and is not a substitute for local policy, trust-approved guidance or individual clinical judgement. Verify drug vesicancy, thresholds and escalation routes against your own organisation's documents before use.
