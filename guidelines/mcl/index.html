Mantle Cell Lymphoma guideline

This guideline has been withdrawn from active use after a control review identified inconsistent version, publication-state and treatment-access information across its HTML, DOCX, PDF and visual formats.

Do not use this withdrawn version for treatment selection, prescribing, commissioning, consent or referral decisions.

The previous downloadable DOCX, PDF and visual algorithms have been withdrawn from active use because they did not form a concordant controlled release family.

Until the controlled replacement is published, use current BSH guidance, NICE and NHS England criteria for England, the current Summary of Product Characteristics, local SACT and transplant policies, and specialist MDT judgement. Access in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland requires separate verification.
